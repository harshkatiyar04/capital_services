Insurance Repair Specialists — demo site update

Version: 1.1.0  |  Release date: 30 July 2026

Prepared for: Capital Services Ltd

Environment: Static site (Vite) — ready for Vercel deployment

Executive Summary

This release delivers a brand-aligned Capital Services marketing website with six core pages, insurer-focused content, interactive service coverage, leadership profiles, and verified Google customer testimonials. The site reflects the updated positioning Insurance Repair Specialists with the strapline From restoration through to complete reinstatements, and incorporates trust metrics supplied by the leadership team.

Release Highlights

  • Homepage hero and metadata updated to Insurance Repair Specialists positioning.
  • Trust metrics banner populated with verified business statistics.
  • Real Google reviews added to Testimonials and homepage review carousel.
  • LV= logo added to the insurer partner marquee.
  • Directors section expanded with Terry Hallahan, Business Development Director.
  • Interactive postcode coverage map and office location maps on About and Contact.
  • Emergency Line call-to-action removed from navigation and contact layouts.
  • Coverage map styling refined to brand amber palette.

Technical Notes

  • Stack: Static HTML, CSS, JavaScript (Vite build).
  • Maps: Leaflet.js with Carto basemap tiles.
  • Hosting: Configured for Vercel (vercel.json, output dist/).
  • No backend required for core site delivery; optional Groq API for review-draft generation on Contact.

Known Considerations

  • Google review text is sourced from publicly available customer feedback; full reviews are available via the linked Google Business profile.
  • Trust metrics should be reviewed periodically and updated if business figures change.
  • Some team photographs rely on LinkedIn-sourced assets where a public headshot is unavailable.
