Cookie Policy

This page explains how Capital Services (Watford) Ltd uses cookies on capitalservices.com. It was last updated on 19 October 2026.

Who we are

Capital Services (Watford) Ltd, Unit 9, Kings Park Industrial Estate, Primrose Hill, Kings Langley, Hertfordshire, WD4 8ST. Phone 01923 270022. Email info@capitalservices.com.

Your choice

When you first visit, we ask you to accept or reject analytics cookies. Both choices are equal. The site works either way. We do not set analytics cookies before you accept.

You can change your mind at any time. Open in the footer, then choose Accept or Reject. Rejecting removes the analytics cookies already stored for this site.

We keep your choice for six months, then ask again.

Cookies we use

We do not use these cookies for advertising, and we do not sell the information.

What analytics is for

If you accept, Google Analytics 4 receives pages you view, a rough location derived from your connection, the browser you use, and how you arrived. We use that to understand which parts of the site are useful. Google’s own description of the service is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Other storage

Pages with an office map remember the map style you last chose in this browser. That is stored on your device and is not an analytics cookie. The internal team and office pages store a sign-in token so staff stay signed in. Those pages are not part of the public site and do not use this banner.

Your browser

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block every cookie, we cannot remember a rejection, so the question will appear again on the next visit.
