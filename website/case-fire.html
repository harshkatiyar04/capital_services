The Full Picture

The Challenge

A Kitchen Fire That Spread Through Smoke

An accidental cooking fire in this St Albans semi-detached left the kitchen a write-off. Heat damage compromised cupboards, worktops, and the ceiling void, while soot and smoke contamination tracked through the hallway, dining room, and first-floor bedrooms.

Electrics needed isolation, soft furnishings were odour-affected, and the family required clear communication with their insurer while living in temporary accommodation. Speed of containment mattered as much as the quality of the eventual rebuild.

Our Approach

Contain First. Rebuild Properly.

Our crew secured the property, contained soot migration, and completed a full fire and smoke investigation pack for the insurer within the first week. Controlled strip-out and professional soot cleaning cleared the way for structural and electrical checks before reinstatement began.

Nine trades worked to a single programme under one lead surveyor. Nine weeks later, the kitchen, finishes, and affected rooms were handed back to pre-loss condition — with full documentation for the insurer throughout.

Scope of Works

Project Timeline

Every phase managed, documented, and delivered on schedule.

Phase 01
Make-Safe & Containment
Property secured, electrics isolated, and soot migration contained to stop further contamination of unaffected rooms.
Phase 02
Fire & Smoke Investigation
Full photographic survey, cause and extent report, and schedule of works agreed with the insurer and the loss adjuster.
Phase 03
Strip-Out & Soot Clean
Damaged kitchen units, ceilings, and finishes removed. Professional soot cleaning and odour treatment across affected rooms.
Phase 04
Structural & Electrical Works
Heat-affected structure checked and made good. Full electrical testing, remedial wiring, and certification before finishes.
Phase 05
Reinstatement
New kitchen, plastering, flooring, decoration, and joinery reinstated to match pre-loss specification and insurer agreement.
Phase 06
Handback
Final snagging, client walkthrough, and formal handback with complete documentation for the homeowners and their insurer.
