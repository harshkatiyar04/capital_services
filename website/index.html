Good to know

Questions we get asked

What do you repair?

We take insured homes and commercial buildings from the first make-safe through to handing them back. The work covers escape of water, flood, fire and smoke, storm, subsidence and impact.

Who do you work with?

Insurers, property owners and their partners. We validate the claim, make the property safe, then carry it through drying and reinstatement. Where a policyholder would rather not use a nominated contractor, we manage the cash settlement instead.

Where do you cover?

London, the Home Counties and the South Coast, from offices in Watford, Yateley and Kings Langley. The coverage map shows the postcodes we attend.

How do I reach you?

Call 01923 270022 or email info@capitalservices.com. The offices are open Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 6:00pm, and every enquiry gets a reply within 24 hours.
