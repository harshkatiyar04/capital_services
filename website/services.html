Domestic & Commercial Claims Management

End-to-end management across residential and commercial property

We provide end-to-end management of both domestic and commercial insurance claims across a wide range of property types.

While the majority of our work involves residential properties, we also have extensive experience managing claims involving commercial offices, industrial units, retail premises, buy-to-let portfolios and other commercial assets.

Emergency Make Safe & Drying Services

Rapid emergency response and professional drying

Following escape of water, flooding or other insured events, making a property safe quickly is essential.

Our emergency response teams attend promptly to remove hazards such as collapsed ceilings, water-damaged materials and debris, protecting both the property and its occupants.

With substantial stocks of professional drying equipment across our regional branches, our specialist technicians can begin the drying process immediately, helping minimise further damage and reduce overall claim costs.

Leak Detection

Non-invasive technology to locate hidden leaks accurately

Identifying the source of a hidden leak can often be one of the most challenging aspects of an insurance claim.

Our specialist leak detection engineers use the latest non-invasive technology and years of practical experience to locate leaks quickly and accurately. By identifying the source with minimal disruption, we help reduce unnecessary damage, speed up repairs and lower overall claim costs for our clients.
