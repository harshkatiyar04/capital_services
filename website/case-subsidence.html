The Full Picture

The Challenge

When the Ground Moves Beneath the Home

Progressive cracking appeared across the rear elevation and internal walls of this Chelmsford detached house. Doors jammed, floors went out of level, and the homeowners faced a long uncertainty period while Admiral investigated the cause.

Clay soils, drainage issues, and tree influence all needed to be ruled in or out. The family wanted a single contractor who could manage monitoring, engineer design, and the eventual structural and cosmetic works without handing them between firms.

Our Approach

Diagnose Fully. Stabilise Permanently.

We coordinated investigation and crack monitoring, then worked with the appointed engineer on an underpinning and drainage scheme that Admiral could approve with confidence. Site works followed a clear sequence so structural stability came before decorative repairs.

David Chen led seven trades through a 22-week programme — underpinning, drainage remediation, internal repairs, and decoration — handing the property back structurally sound and finished to pre-loss condition.

Project Timeline

Every phase managed, documented, and delivered on schedule.

Phase 01
Investigation & Monitoring
Crack monitoring, site investigation, and cause analysis coordinated with Admiral and the appointed engineer.
Phase 02
Engineer Design
Underpinning and drainage design prepared, costed, and approved before structural works commenced on site.
Phase 03
Underpinning
Foundations stabilised with engineered underpinning to arrest further movement and restore structural capacity.
Phase 04
Drainage Remediation
Defects in drainage contributing to ground softening repaired and tested to protect the newly stabilised foundations.
Phase 05
Internal Repairs
Cracked masonry, plaster, joinery, and floors made good once structural works were complete and signed off.
Phase 06
Decoration & Sign-Off
Full decoration, final inspection, and formal handback with Admiral documentation and engineer certification complete.
