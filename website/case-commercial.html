The Full Picture

The Challenge

A Flooded Floor and a Business Still Running

A burst supply line flooded the ground-floor office suite at Watford Business Park overnight. Carpets, raised-access flooring, partitions, and M&E were compromised across open-plan workstations, meeting rooms, and the reception zone.

The facilities manager needed staff back as quickly as possible, Allianz needed a clear scope, and downtime had to be managed with a phased reopen rather than a single end-of-project handback.

Our Approach

Salvage Fast. Reopen in Phases.

We made the floor safe, salvaged recoverable assets, and strip-out began within days. Industrial drying and M&E remediation ran to a programme that released zones for fit-out as soon as moisture and electrical clearances allowed.

Priya Sharma coordinated eleven trades across a twelve-week claim. Reception and one wing reopened mid-programme; the remaining suites followed in sequence so the business never faced a full twelve-week shutdown.

Scope of Works

Project Timeline

Every phase managed, documented, and delivered on schedule.

Phase 01
Make-Safe & Salvage
Electrics isolated, standing water extracted, and recoverable furniture and equipment salvaged for the facilities team.
Phase 02
Controlled Strip-Out
Saturated flooring, damaged partitions, and affected finishes removed to expose structure for drying and M&E works.
Phase 03
Industrial Drying
Monitored drying programme with daily readings, enabling zones to be released for fit-out as soon as they validated dry.
Phase 04
M&E Remediation
Electrical, data, and mechanical systems tested, remediated, and certified ahead of phased occupancy.
Phase 05
Phased Fit-Out
Flooring, partitions, ceilings, and finishes reinstated zone by zone so reception and workspaces could reopen early.
Phase 06
Handover
Final snagging, facilities walkthrough, and formal handover with Allianz documentation for the completed suite.
